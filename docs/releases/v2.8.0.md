# VoxPlane 2.8.0

## 更新摘要

- 在同一序列与时相的另一层原位粘贴 ROI，保留轮廓并用目标层像素重新计算统计。
- 导出前可逐帧核对画面和烧录文字标记，查看所选帧、源文件和实际输出数量。
- 2D 与平铺视图保留小数窗宽精度，派生 CT 的未知单位会明确显示。
- 3D 画面就绪后才启用截图；设置支持按命令名查找快捷键。

## Update summary

- Paste an ROI in place on another slice of the same series and phase, preserving its contour and measuring the target pixels.
- Review frames and burned-in text flags before export, with separate counts for selected frames, source files and outputs.
- Keep fractional window widths in 2D and montage views, and see when a derived CT image has unspecified units.
- Capture 3D screenshots once the view is ready and find keyboard shortcuts by command name in Settings.

## 中文说明

### 测量与阅片

复制矩形、椭圆或自由手绘 ROI 后，在同序列、同时相且几何匹配的另一张原始二维切片按 ⇧⌘V（Windows 为 Ctrl+Shift+V），也可使用测量面板或右键菜单。新 ROI 独立可编辑，按目标层有效像素重算均值、总体标准差及极值。普通粘贴继续偏移副本。FWHM 的结果与导出报告显示本次计算实际使用的方法。

### 核对导出内容

导出对话框可核对患者、检查及序列，逐帧按原始分辨率预览。PNG 仅写出所选帧；DICOM 保留所涉及的完整源文件，可能包含其他时相或回波。元数据匿名化后，像素中仍可能留有文字和面部信息，请在共享前核对画面。[本版导出说明](https://github.com/junforge-dev/voxplane/blob/v2.8.0/docs/export.md)

### 安装及限制

升级前请保存正在编辑的工作区。macOS 提供 Apple Silicon DMG，使用 ad-hoc 签名，未经 Apple 公证。Windows x64 提供安装版和便携版，均未做 Authenticode 签名。系统可能提示无法验证开发者或发布者。

## English details

### Measurement and viewing

Copy a rectangular, elliptical or freehand ROI. On a compatible original 2D slice of the same series and phase, choose the in-place command from the measurement panel or context menu, or press ⇧⌘V on macOS or Ctrl+Shift+V on Windows. The new ROI is independently editable. Its mean, population standard deviation and range come from the target slice. Ordinary paste keeps its offset. FWHM results and exported reports identify the method used for the calculation.

### Reviewing an export

Check the patient, study and series, then preview individual frames at native resolution. PNG writes the selected frames. DICOM retains entire source files, which can include other phases or echoes. Text and faces may remain visible in the pixels after metadata anonymization. Review the images before sharing them.

### Installation and limitations

Save your workspace before upgrading. The Apple Silicon DMG uses an ad-hoc signature and has no Apple notarization. Both the Windows x64 installer and portable EXE lack Authenticode signatures. Your operating system may show an unverified developer or publisher warning. [Changes since 2.7.0](https://github.com/junforge-dev/voxplane/compare/v2.7.0...v2.8.0)
